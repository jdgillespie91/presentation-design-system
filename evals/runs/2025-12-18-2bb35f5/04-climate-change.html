The Basics

What is Climate Change?

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns. While some changes are natural, human activities have been the main driver since the 1800s, primarily through burning fossil fuels like coal, oil, and gas.

These activities release greenhouse gases that trap heat in Earth's atmosphere, causing the planet to warm at an unprecedented rate.

Root Causes

What's Driving Climate Change?

Energy Production

Burning coal, oil, and natural gas for electricity and heat accounts for about 25% of global emissions.

Transportation

Cars, trucks, ships, and planes running on fossil fuels contribute roughly 16% of greenhouse gas emissions.

Deforestation

Cutting down forests reduces nature's ability to absorb CO2 and releases stored carbon back into the atmosphere.

Consequences

How Climate Change Affects Us

Extreme Weather Events

More frequent and intense hurricanes, droughts, floods, and heatwaves

Rising Sea Levels

Threatening coastal communities and low-lying island nations

Ecosystem Disruption

Species extinction, coral bleaching, and disrupted migration patterns

Food & Water Security

Crop failures, water shortages, and increased hunger in vulnerable regions

Taking Action

Pathways to a Sustainable Future

Renewable Energy

Transitioning to solar, wind, and other clean energy sources to power our world

Conservation

Protecting forests, oceans, and ecosystems that naturally absorb carbon

Innovation

Developing new technologies for carbon capture and sustainable living

Personal Impact

What You Can Do

At Home

Switch to energy-efficient appliances, reduce water waste, and consider renewable energy options for your home.

Transportation

Walk, bike, or use public transit when possible. Consider electric vehicles for your next car purchase.

Consumer Choices

Buy local, reduce meat consumption, minimize single-use plastics, and support sustainable brands.

Civic Engagement

Vote for climate-conscious leaders, support climate policies, and participate in local environmental initiatives.
